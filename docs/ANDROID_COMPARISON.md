## Certification evidence

The repository records an authentication mismatch and incomplete behavior analysis, not evidence that QR was accepted or that certification was completed. On 2026-10-12, [Roku rendezvous documentation](https://developer.roku.com/dev/docs/authentication-and-linking) still limits off-device linking to TVE cable/satellite apps and requires other apps to authenticate on-device. [Authenticated app testing](https://developer.roku.com/dev/docs/authenticated-cert-testing) requires working sign-in/out automation, test credentials, deep links and the other listed prerequisites. This app's classification/provider integration remains unresolved. Android QR functionality does not resolve that decision.
