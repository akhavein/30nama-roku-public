# Store listing draft

Name: **30nama - Unofficial**

Short description:

> An unofficial client for existing 30nama accounts. Browse, search, resume movies and episodes, and choose available audio and subtitles with remote-friendly controls. A valid paid account is required for subscription content.

Long description:

> Watch content available to your existing 30nama account using a native TV interface. Search in English or Persian, continue a movie or episode, browse seasons, and manage your TV and account Watchlists. Customize playback controls, subtitles and optional sleep settings. Provider-marked intros can be skipped manually. Scene previews depend on source compatibility and service availability.
>
> This independent client is not endorsed by or affiliated with 30nama or Roku. It does not sell subscriptions or include media. Content access, availability and permitted regions depend on your provider account.

Original identity assets: `images/icon-fhd.png` (Store poster, 540×405), `images/icon-hd.png` (290×218), HD/SD/FHD splash images. No provider logo or film still is embedded. `scripts/generate-store-art.py` reproduces these using Pillow and the bundled OFL font.

Screenshots: **not captured or fabricated**. Obtain genuine approved app UI captures on an available test device; artwork files are not screenshots. Publisher still supplies territories, rating, contacts, privacy/terms URLs and test-account access through the Developer Dashboard.
